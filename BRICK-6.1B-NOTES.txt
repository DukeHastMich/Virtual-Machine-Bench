WAVETABLE BRICK 6.1B - CONTINUOUS WINMM OUTPUT

Symptom:
Wavetable music was recognizable but sounded like a blown speaker after Brick 6.1.

Cause corrected:
WinMmStereoOut16 still inherited Brick 6's old nonblocking presentation rule.  When
all eight 10 ms waveOut buffers were busy, QueueBuffer discarded the next complete
480-frame PCM block.  Repeated 10 ms discontinuities sound like crackle/distortion.

Change:
- QueueBuffer now waits for a completed WinMM buffer on the DEDICATED SB16 host-audio worker.
- The 80286 / machine-owner thread still never waits on waveOut.
- No guest-visible DSP, DMA, IRQ, MPU, OPL-register, or MIDI timing behavior changed.
- No IDE/storage files changed.
- No synth/SoundFont behavior changed.

Apply over Brick 6.1 Audio Clock (and keep the HDD hot-trace patch independently if desired).
