Brick 6 compiler fix

Visual Basic error:
BC30668: 'Span(Of Single)' is obsolete: 'Types with embedded references are not supported in this version of your compiler.'

Cause:
Brick 6 declared Span(Of Single) locals in WavetableRenderer.vb.

Fix:
- Removed all explicit Span(Of Single) locals.
- Use MeltySynth's Single() Render overload for complete work blocks.
- For a short final block, reuse cached exact-length Single() tail buffers so the
  synth advances by exactly the requested number of PCM frames instead of rendering
  the unused remainder of the 512-frame host work buffer.
- No guest-visible MIDI, IRQ, DMA, timing, or card-register behavior was changed.

Apply this corrected Brick 6 over Brick 5 / the original Brick 6.
